# Claim register

## Permitted if gates pass

- The implementation explicitly maintains multiple executable transition-and-goal
  hypotheses instead of committing to one program.
- Its action-specific disagreement is converted into expected decision improvement.
- Its full controller values information across remaining level weights and outperforms
  the preregistered same-backbone comparator on the stated evaluation.
- The design is reproducible with a local open-weight model and offline Kaggle runtime.

The final two performance/reproducibility sentences are prohibited until their named
gates and a network-disabled full rehearsal pass. As of 12 July 2026, no performance gate
has passed and no private score exists.

## Never claim

- “First” without a final dated literature audit.
- A calibrated Bayesian posterior.
- General hidden-state identification.
- Public-game performance as genuine unseen generalization.
- ARC-AGI-2 as evidence for active exploration.
- A positive effect if either development or confirmation claim gate fails.

## Closest prior work

- Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3, arXiv:2605.05138.
- Explore Before You Solve / AERA, arXiv:2605.25931.
- OPINE-World, arXiv:2607.01531.

The narrow contrast is persistent multi-program uncertainty plus remaining-level-weighted
decision value under fixed action/token/runtime budgets.

The dated comparison and weekly literature-audit template live in
`docs/prior_work_matrix.md`; update it through code freeze before changing any novelty
sentence.

The formal threshold is exact only for the finite one-query transfer model stated in
`docs/theory.md`. The empirical multiplier is not claimed to be a Bellman-optimal value
function for ARC-AGI-3.
